02

Lead alerts where you already work

Moments after the call ends, you get the name, number, what they wanted, and the full transcript. Email, Slack, or Google Chat on Basic. Email plus SMS to your phone on Pro (after A2P approval). A mobile-friendly dashboard is available anytime if you want to dig in; no app to install.

Hours, prices, location, services. Your AI handles them every time, day or night, in either language, so your team stays focused on actual work.

Google Calendar support available. Microsoft Outlook support is on the roadmap. Pro tier only. We don't pretend to integrate with platforms we don't.
